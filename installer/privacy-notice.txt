Tapedeck is an unofficial YouTube Music client. It is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

PRIVACY

Tapedeck has no servers or analytics of its own. It sends nothing to its developers.

To do what you ask, it connects to:

  - YouTube Music and Google, to browse, search and play music, and to sign
    in if you choose to. Google's privacy policy applies.

  - LRCLIB (lrclib.net), only while the Lyrics panel is open, to find
    time-synced lyrics. It receives the song's title, artist, album and
    length, never account details. You can turn this off in Settings.

Tapedeck never sees your Google password. You type it into Google's own page.
Your sign-in session is kept on this PC, encrypted with your Windows account.
You can choose not to stay signed in. Uninstalling offers to delete it.

Full policy: https://github.com/bhaiyatu/tapedeck/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
